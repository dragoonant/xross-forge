# Rulings log — xross-forge

One line per ruling: `RULING R-NNN: <rule question> | <what the engine does> | <why, with source> | status`.

- **confirmed** = the Playing Manual (or card text) answers it explicitly; logged because it is a frequent doubt.
- **default** = manual silent or implicit; the engine takes the reading most consistent with the manual's examples. Change only with a new source.
- **open** = genuinely uncertain and consequential; the engine implements the default shown but the item needs an official answer (official FAQ/Q&A, judge, or a newer manual).

Citation keys as in `docs/rules.md` (PM p.N = printed page number). Seeded at M0 on 2026-10-29 from PM 2026-04-09, QM 2025-08-13, FR v1.0.4, LB 2026-07-13 and the API card text. The official FAQ (`docs/sources/api/faq.json`, 136 entries, fetched 2026-10-29) was checked by the coordinator for the seven open rulings the same day: R-008, R-010, R-025 and R-031 are now confirmed by FAQ entries; the other `default` lines still need a pass against the FAQ in M1.

## Attacks and targeting

RULING R-001: Can one leader attack more than once per turn? | Yes: every attack card played is a new attack and any non-downed own leader may be its attacker, including one that already attacked or just awakened. | PM p.07 note "you may attack any number of times with the same leader", QM. | status: confirmed
RULING R-002: Can attacks or effects target a downed leader? | No, neither as attacker, attack target, effect target nor damage recipient, unless the card text explicitly refers to downed leaders (復活ポータル, 復活ビーコン). | PM p.08; PM p.17 Q10. | status: confirmed
RULING R-003: Does the attacker awaken once per attack or once per downed leader? | Once per attack: after all 【アタック後】, if ≥1 opposing leader was downed during the attack and the attacker is not awakened, it awakens and its 【覚醒時】 resolves once. | PM p.12 step 4 ("1体以上"). | status: confirmed
RULING R-004: Is awakening immediate on the down, or deferred? | Deferred to after every 【アタック後】 has resolved; any 【アタック後】 condition such as "アタッカーが覚醒しているなら" sees the pre-awakening state. | PM p.12 order (step 3 then step 4). | status: confirmed
RULING R-005: Overkill — what happens to damage beyond remaining HP? | The excess is lost (counters are cleared on down). The engine records, per attack, `excess = damage dealt by 【アタックする】 − attacked leader's remaining HP before it` for the オーバーキルN keyword; excess from 【アタック後】 damage is not overkill. | PM p.08 (counters removed on down); keyword reminder text "このアタックを受けたリーダーに…残り体力よりもN以上大きいダメージを与えていたなら" (API, 15 cards). | status: default **Review 2026-10-29:** FAQ #115 says an 【アタック後】 effect that downs the attacked leader does trigger later オーバーキル checks, so excess must also count 【アタック後】 damage to the attacked leader; no ST01/ST02 card reads excess, fix before the first Overkill card (TODO.md). **Revised 2026-10-30 (engine, R-063):** the excess is now the total damage the attack and its effects dealt to the attacked leader minus its remaining HP before the attack, 【アタック後】 damage included (FAQ #44, #115); the sentence "excess from 【アタック後】 damage is not overkill" above is withdrawn.

## Engine groundwork for the booster pool (2026-10-30)

RULING R-063: How is Overkill measured, and when is it read? | Overkill N is true when the damage the attack and its effects have dealt to the attacked leader so far exceeds the remaining HP that leader had BEFORE the attack by N or more. It is read when the 【アタック後】 effect resolves, so damage from an earlier 【アタック後】 of the same attack counts, and a down by Crossfire-style effects counts as 0 damage. Logged as `overkill` when it fires. | FAQ #44 (remaining HP before the attack: 100 left, Overkill 30 needs 130), FAQ #115 (a later Overkill triggers after an earlier 【アタック後】 downed the leader). | status: confirmed
RULING R-064: Looking at the top N of a deck when it has fewer than N cards, and who sees what. | The deck-out procedure runs once first (the remaining cards stay on top, the shuffled face-down trash goes under them, one random tactics card is lost, or the match is lost with an empty tactics deck), then the top N are looked at, so the cards that were left are part of them. Cards looked at on your own deck are visible to you only (the opponent's view carries counts); "公開する" shows them to both players; looking at the opponent's deck and revealing it trashes into THEIR trash. The looked-at cards stay on the deck until a later step moves them; a card not moved stays where it is. | FAQ #109, #117, #119 (the procedure and the worked example), FAQ #8 (reveal = every player sees it), PM p.14 Q1. | status: confirmed (hidden-ness: default)
RULING R-065: In what order do the effects right of 【アタックする】 and the damage happen? | After the attacker and the attacked leader are chosen: first the card's effects top to bottom ("ダメージ+N" among them, each reading its condition at its turn), then the damage is dealt, then 【アタック後】.
