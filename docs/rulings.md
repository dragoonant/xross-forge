# Rulings log — xross-forge

One line per ruling: `RULING R-NNN: <rule question> | <what the engine does> | <why, with source> | status`.

- **confirmed** = the Playing Manual (or card text) answers it explicitly; logged because it is a frequent doubt.
- **default** = manual silent or implicit; the engine takes the reading most consistent with the manual's examples. Change only with a new source.
- **open** = genuinely uncertain and consequential; the engine implements the default shown but the item needs an official answer (official FAQ/Q&A, judge, or a newer manual).

Citation keys as in `docs/rules.md` (PM p.N = printed page number). Seeded at M0 on 2026-10-29 from PM 2026-04-09, QM 2025-08-13, FR v1.0.4, LB 2026-07-13 and the API card text. The official FAQ (`docs/sources/api/faq.json`, 136 entries, fetched 2026-10-29) was checked by the coordinator for the seven open rulings the same day: R-008, R-010, R-025 and R-031 are now confirmed by FAQ entries; the other `default` lines still need a pass against the FAQ in M1.

## Attacks and targeting

RULING R-001: Can one leader attack more than once per turn? | Yes: every attack card played is a new attack and any non-downed own leader may be its attacker, including one that already attacked or just awakened. | PM p.07 note "you may attack any number of times with the same leader", QM. | status: confirmed
RULING R-002: Can attacks or effects target a downed leader? | No, neither as attacker, attack target, effect target nor damage recipient, unless the card text explicitly refers to downed leaders (復活ポータル, 復活ビーコン). | PM p.08; PM p.17 Q10. | status: confirmed
RULING R-003: Does the attacker awaken once per attack or once per downed leader? | Once per attack: after all 【アタック後】, if ≥1 opposing leader was downed during the attack and the attacker is not awakened, it awakens and its 【覚醒時】 resolves once. | PM p.12 step 4 ("1体以上"). | status: confirmed
RULING R-004: Is awakening immediate on the down, or deferred? | Deferred to after every 【アタック後】 has resolved; any 【アタック後】 condition such as "アタッカーが覚醒しているなら" sees the pre-awakening state. | PM p.12 order (step 3 then step 4). | status: confirmed
RULING R-005: Overkill — what happens to damage beyond remaining HP? | The excess is lost (counters are cleared on down). The engine records, per attack, `excess = damage dealt by 【アタックする】 − attacked leader's remaining HP before it` for the オーバーキルN keyword; excess from 【アタック後】 damage is not overkill. | PM p.08 (counters removed on down); keyword reminder text "このアタックを受けたリーダーに…残り体力よりもN以上大きいダメージを与えていたなら" (API, 15 cards). | status: default **Review 2026-10-29:** FAQ #115 says an 【アタック後】 effect that downs the attacked leader does trigger later オーバーキル checks, so excess must also count 【アタック後】 damage to the attacked leader; no ST01/ST02 card reads excess, fix before the first Overkill card (TODO.md).
RULING R-006: Do downs caused outside an attack awaken anyone (memoria/tactics 【プレイ時】 damage, 【覚醒時】 damage, 攻撃要請)? | No. Only the attacker of an attack awakens, plus effects that say "覚醒させる". A 【覚醒時】 that downs further leaders causes no second awakening. | PM p.08 and p.12 tie awakening to "the attack" and its 【アタック後】; no other source named. | status: default
RULING R-007: Does a down caused by the attack card's own 【プレイ時】 (step 1 of the PM p.12 sequence, before 【アタックする】) count for the attacker's awakening? | Yes: the engine counts every opposing down that happens from step 1 to step 3 of that attack card's resolution. | PM p.12 step 4 says "if one or more opposing leaders have gone down" without restricting to steps 2–3; but PM p.08 says "downed by the attack" and the attacker is only chosen in step 2. No current card damages in an attack card's 【プレイ時】 (API), so the impact is nil until one is printed. | FAQ #111 (analogous): a down caused by a memoria's 【プレイ時】 played via an 【アタック後】 effect does not awaken anyone, because it resolves after the attack has fully finished; no FAQ entry on an attack card's own 【プレイ時】. | status: open
RULING R-008: "先攻の1ターン目" tactics restriction — match's first turn only, or each round? | Each round: the player who goes first in a round cannot play a tactics card on their first turn of that round. | PM p.11 redefines 先攻 every round ("the loser becomes 先攻"); PM p.10 states the restriction alongside the per-round tactics placement. | FAQ #49: each round's first player cannot play a tactics card on their first turn. | status: confirmed
RULING R-009: Does playing the PP Ticket use the turn's one tactics play? | Yes; it is played from the tactics area like any tactics card. | PM p.03 ("special tactics card"), PM p.06/p.10 limit applies to "cards in the tactics area". | status: default
RULING R-010: Does an unplayed tactics card (incl. the PP Ticket) stay in the tactics area into the next round? | Yes. Next-round setup then adds one more face-down card, so the area can hold 2+ cards; still only 1 play per turn. | QM round-end note lists "tactics area, trash, equipment and awakened state stay as they are"; PM p.11 cleanup moves only play area and hand and never mentions the tactics area. | FAQ #50: unplayed tactics cards stay in the tactics area and can be played in later rounds; next-round setup still adds one face-down card. | status: confirmed
RULING R-011: Do damage counters stay on a leader when it awakens? | Yes. Awakening changes printed ATK/HP (e.g. 110→140); counters are untouched, so remaining HP rises by the difference. | PM p.08 describes flip + 【覚醒時】 only; counters are removed only on down/heal/round end (PM p.08, p.11, p.16 Q9). | status: default
RULING R-012: Does equipment stay on a leader that goes down, and can equipment be attached to a downed leader? | Stays attached (and keeps working once revived); cannot be newly attached to a downed leader (no targeting downed leaders). | 復活ポータル/復活ビーコン text trashes a revived leader's equipment (PM p.17 Q10), implying it stays through the down; PM p.11 keeps equipment across rounds; PM p.08 targeting ban. | status: default
RULING R-013: A leader's HP falls (equipment moved off by メカニカルエキスパート, base-HP change) to at or below its damage counters — what happens? | It is downed immediately; nobody awakens (not an attack down) — consistent with R-006. | PM p.12 note "a leader with counters ≥ HP is downed at once". | status: default
RULING R-014: How long does an unused Attack Boost last? | Until it is consumed by your next attack or the card leaves the play area (end phase, round end). An Echo card replayed at the next main-phase start provides a fresh boost. | PM p.09 (next attack only; play-area cards trashed at end phase); Echo reminder text (API BP04-045). | status: default
RULING R-015: Does a consumable tactics card's 【アタック強化】 follow the same "next attack" rule as memoria? | Yes, by play-area order. | PM p.10 (face up in the play area makes 【アタック強化】 work), PM p.12 step 2-2. | status: confirmed
RULING R-016: Can the damage value go below 0 after "damage −"? | No; floor at 0, and 0 damage places no counters. | PM p.12 mentions "damage −" without a floor; a negative damage has no meaning. | status: default
RULING R-017: Healing above max HP / recovering more PP than spent? | Capped: heal removes at most the counters present; PP recovery untaps at most the sideways PP cards. No excess is stored. | PM p.16 Q9. | status: confirmed
RULING R-018: What if both players' leaders are all downed during the same resolution (e.g. a self-damage effect while pending opposing downs resolve)? | Resolution is strictly sequential; the player whose team became fully downed **first** loses the round. A single atomic event downing both teams cannot occur with current card text (no effect damages both sides). | PM p.12 round end triggers "when either player's leaders are all downed"; PM p.17 Q11 (simultaneous match loss = both lose) covers the match, not the round. | FAQ #5 (simultaneous match loss: both players lose) covers the match only; no FAQ entry on a simultaneous round loss as of 2026-10-29. | status: open
RULING R-019: Does an effect that shuffles the trash into the deck (アイテムショップ) cost a tactics card? | No; the penalty belongs only to the deck-shortfall procedure. | PM p.14 Q1 ties the penalty to "the deck lacks cards when needed"; card text (API BP04-076) has no penalty. | status: default
RULING R-020: When is the 7-card hand limit enforced, and who chooses the discards? | Only at end-phase step 3; the hand owner chooses; discarded cards go to the trash face down. Hands above 7 during the turn are legal. | PM p.06 step 3; PM p.04 trash face-down rule. | status: default
RULING R-021: Who makes choices in effects? | The player whose card it is chooses targets/orders; "対戦相手は手札をN枚捨てる" = the opponent chooses their own discards; "ランダムに" = engine RNG; "すべてのプレイヤーは…" = turn player first, then opponent. The opponent has no other actions on your turn. | PM p.06 (only the turn player acts), PM p.12/Q6 (turn player orders); manual silent on the rest. | status: default
RULING R-022: Whose play area is "プレイエリア" in card text without "自分の"? | The card controller's own play area. | Each player has a separate play area (PM p.04, SHEET); 復活ビーコン says "自分のプレイエリア" explicitly; cards persisting across turns (Echo, 【ラウンド中】) make the question live. | FAQ #92, #102: 「プレイエリア」 in card text means your own play area. | status: confirmed
RULING R-023: What do "他のリーダー" and "このアタックを受けたリーダー" refer to on a memoria/tactics 【アタック後】 or an equipment-granted 【アタック後】? | The attack that the effect is resolving for (the attack the card boosted / the equipped attacker's attack). "他" = not the leader that received that attack. | PM p.14 Q2–Q3, p.15 Q6 (超新星), p.16 Q8. | status: confirmed
RULING R-024: Echo (エコー) details. | At the owner's end phase step 1, an upright Echo card in the play area turns sideways instead of being trashed; at their next main-phase start a sideways Echo card is replayed free (its 【プレイ時】 resolves; its boost applies to the next attack) and stays sideways; at the next end phase it is sideways, so it is trashed. At round end it is trashed like any play-area card. It counts as a play-area card for counting conditions while present. | Echo reminder text (API BP04-045 etc.); PM p.11 cleanup. | status: default
RULING R-025: A card with several 【アタックする】 (ストームラッシュ): boosts, awakening, "このアタック"? | Each 【アタックする】 is a separate attack with its own attacker/target. Pending 【アタック強化】 boosts apply to the first attack only (FAQ #82). **Awakening is checked after each attack that downed an opposing leader, before the next attack** (FAQ #81: an attack is choose attacker/target → compute damage → deal damage → 【アタック後】). Cards played by another card's 【アタック後】 resolve after the whole multi-attack card finishes (FAQ #114). | Reminder text on BP03-017; FAQ #81, #82, #114 answer the questions directly. | status: confirmed
RULING R-026: "このアタックを受けたリーダーはダウンする" (クロスファイア, 魔王降臨) — when, and does it count? | Applied during 【アタックする】 after the damage step if the condition holds; the leader is downed (counters cleared). It counts as downed by the attack: the attacker awakens and "このアタックを受けたリーダーがダウンしているなら" is true. Overkill excess = 0. | PM p.14 Q3 (attack-step downs count), PM p.12 step 2. Exact placement before/after damage is manual silent. | status: default
RULING R-027: Order of effects when several cards trigger. | Within a card: top to bottom. Several 【アタック後】 from different sources: the turn player picks the order, one at a time, conditions checked at resolution. Cards played by an effect during an attack go to the far right of the play area (to the right of any card already there) and resolve after the attack ends, left to right. | PM p.07, p.09, p.12, p.15 Q6; reminder text on 一騎当千/三銃士/リンク・アサルト (API). | status: confirmed FAQ #70: new cards go to the far right; play-area cards resolve left to right.
RULING R-028: Do equipment-granted 【アタック後】 abilities (オートタレット, 盗賊キット, ヒーリングオーブ) join the turn player's ordering pool? | Yes, whenever the equipped leader is the attacker; ordered freely with the card 【アタック後】 effects. | PM p.12 "ordered by the turn player" for multiple 【アタック後】; equipment text "this leader has the following ability" (API). | status: default
RULING R-029: "この効果はターンに1回しか発動しない" — per card copy or per name? | Per source (each equipment card instance) per turn. | Card text (API BP02-077..079); manual silent. | status: default
RULING R-030: Enforce Limited/BAN? | Only in "sanctioned" deck-validation mode; casual mode allows starter decks as printed (incl. エナジーチャージャー). "Neutral card" in LB is treated as an attack/memoria without a `require_leader` build rule, for display only. | LB applies to official/sanctioned events; LB does not define "neutral". | status: default
RULING R-031: Tag checks (「VSPO!」を持つ / 「CR」を持つ): do downed leaders count, and where does the tag come from? | Downed leaders still count (checking a characteristic is not targeting). Tags come from a hand-maintained table (data), not the API. | PM p.02 defines tag as a printed field; PM p.08 forbids targeting downed leaders, not reading them; API has no tag field. | FAQ #10: 「X」を持つ means the tag is X, and downed leaders are included. | status: confirmed (tag source still a hand table, PLAN D13)
RULING R-032: "対戦相手のリーダーすべてにNダメージ" — downed leaders? | Only non-downed leaders take damage. | PM p.08. | status: confirmed
RULING R-033: Who can see face-down tactics cards in the tactics area? | Owner anytime; opponent never (until played). | PM p.05 note (own face-down tactics cards visible to owner at any time). | status: default
RULING R-034: Can a card be played without enough upright PP? | No; a cost must be paid in full by turning PP sideways; costs are after modifiers (電磁スモーク +1, 復活ビーコン −1 per card, floor 1 where printed); a cost-0 card needs no PP. | PM p.06 ("pay PP equal to the cost"). | status: default
RULING R-035: Deck shortfall when the trash has no (or too few) face-down cards. | Run the procedure once per operation: set aside the remaining deck, shuffle the trash's face-down cards in, take the tactics penalty (or lose if the tactics deck is empty), then perform as much of the operation as possible; any remaining shortfall in that same operation is ignored (no second penalty). | PM p.14 Q1 gives one cycle; silent on a second shortfall. | FAQ #109/#117/#119/#124 restate the one-cycle procedure; none covers a second shortfall (checked 2026-10-29). | status: open
RULING R-036: Deck-out during end-phase PP draw or the 4-card round-start draw. | Same procedure as any draw. At round start the first player draws first; if both players hit a tactics-deck loss during the same setup step, both lose (Q11). | PM p.14 Q1 ("drawing" in general), PM p.17 Q11. | status: default
RULING R-037: Discards, hand trashing, look-at-then-trash: face up or down? | Face down (all non-tactics cards); tactics cards always face up. | PM p.04 trash rule, p.06, p.11. | status: default
RULING R-038: May a leader carry several equipment cards? | Yes, no limit; each equipment play still uses the turn's one tactics play. | Manual silent; PM p.10 "attach to any leader you like"; 復活ポータル says "all cards that leader is equipped with" (plural). | FAQ #34: no limit on equipment per leader. | status: confirmed
RULING R-039: Does an equipment card's 【プレイ時】 (ヒーリングオーブ) resolve, given equipment never enters the play area? | Yes, when attached. | Card text (API BP02-079); PM p.17 Q10 card text over rules. | status: default
RULING R-040: Does the first-turn tactics ban touch the second player? | No; the second player may play a tactics card (incl. the PP Ticket) on their first turn of the round. | PM p.06/p.10 restrict only the first player (先攻). | status: confirmed
RULING R-041: Can a heal target a downed leader? | No. | PM p.08 (no targeting downed). | status: confirmed
RULING R-042: A self-inflicted down of your own last leader (e.g. 【プレイ時】 "自分の体力N以上のリーダー…にNダメージを与えてもよい"). | The round ends; the opponent wins it; nobody awakens. | PM p.11 ("either player's leaders all down → round over"); R-006. | status: default

RULING R-053: "このアタックを受けたリーダーがダウンしているなら" when the attacked leader went down in a 【アタック後】 effect, and when a different leader went down. | The condition is true when the attacked leader is down at the moment the effect resolves, however it went down (including damage dealt to it by an earlier 【アタック後】 of the same attack); it is false when only another leader went down. RULING R-054: Mandatory targets: may an effect "Heal 1 of your leaders by N" / "Deal N damage to 1 opposing leader" be declined or aimed at an undamaged leader? | The effect is not optional: when at least one legal target exists the player must choose one (an undamaged leader is a legal heal target and the heal does nothing); with none, the effect does nothing and a `fizzle` is logged. RULING R-056: Discarding when the hand is exactly as large as the discard count. | If the hand holds fewer cards than N, all of it is discarded without a question (the rules say "all", FAQ #47).
