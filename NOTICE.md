It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to Brave group, Game & Co. Inc., SAMURAI KOBO, or any
talent agency, team or talent named in the game. Nothing here is official, and nothing is sold or monetised.

- **No official material is included.** The repository contains no official card art, logos, icons,
  key visuals or audio. Rules text, interface copy and lore are written in our own words.
- **Names and card data are used for reference only.** Card, leader and talent names and card numbers follow
  the publisher's public card list so that players can recognise the cards. They belong to their owners.
- **Illustrations are AI-generated** (#AIイラスト) depictions made for this project from our own descriptions,
  not copies of official art. Sound effects and music are AI-generated too. The announcer is a synthetic voice
  designed from a written description for this project; no real talent's voice is cloned or imitated.
